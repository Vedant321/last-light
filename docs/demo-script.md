# Three-minute demo — top-down game

Choose **Try the 3-minute mission**. It starts with fresh memory and a disclosed 180-second deadline. Walk with WASD or arrow keys; click the floor or a task to pathfind. Gold exclamation markers identify the next useful task. **Walk me there** moves Alex along a valid path but never completes a task. Press **E**, click a nearby object, or use the contextual action button to interact.

1. Walk to the maintenance cabinet. Inspect it, then take the battery.
2. Bring the visible battery left to the Engineering socket and connect it. The hatch opens.
3. Walk inside and speak to Mara to start the bypass. She works for five seconds, then visibly walks to the shuttle.
4. Optionally choose **Demonstrate rewind**. Equipment and doors reset. Open Radio and ask what ECHO remembers; the offline authored reply cites only observed events.
5. Repeat the preparation if you reset. Walk down to the Docking locker and collect portable oxygen.
6. Carry the tank back up and right to Medical’s transfer window. Give it to Imani.
7. Return to the Echo Core in Communications. Read the consequence, then hold its shutdown control for one second.
8. Restore power at the Medical panel. Enter Medical and begin the patient transfer. Imani and Eli visibly move to Docking over five seconds.
9. Walk to the shuttle, board, and launch once all five crew are aboard.

Normal mode has an eight-minute deadline, 25-second bypass installation, ten-second engineer transit, and twenty-second medical transfer. Travel now uses actual player movement and connected corridors, replacing the earlier room-selection transit delays. Demo walking is slightly faster; it never grants equipment, completes a repair, or transfers a crewmate automatically.
